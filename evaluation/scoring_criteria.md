# Evaluation Criteria

We publish our evaluation criteria on purpose: building toward a known, well-specified target is part of the job. This document tells you **what we assess and what earns top marks**. It does not describe our internal scoring or decision process.

Your submission is assessed across three dimensions, weighted to reflect this role's hybrid technical + product focus:

| Dimension | Weight | What it covers |
|-----------|:------:|----------------|
| **Technical Architecture & AI Orchestration** | 40% | LLM orchestration, agentic patterns, grounding, engineering quality |
| **Domain & Data Logic** | 30% | Correct handling of the schema and the two-layer modeling task |
| **Product Thinking** | 30% | Coherence and strategic quality of `blueprint.md` |

Each criterion below lists **what a top-scoring submission looks like**. Aim for these.

---

## Dimension A — Technical Architecture & AI Orchestration *(40%)*

This dimension carries the most weight. A submission that only visualizes or restates data — without genuine orchestration — will score low here.

**A1. LLM orchestration depth.** A clean orchestration layer with well-scoped tools, grounded retrieval over your context model, and structured (parseable) outputs. It handles model limits sensibly (token budget, partial context, guardrails) and would extend to new tools without a rewrite. A single monolithic prompt with the whole dataset pasted in is the anti-pattern.

**A2. Agentic pattern & control flow.** A genuine multi-step loop — detect → reason → decide/act — with explicit state, tool selection driven by reasoning, clear termination conditions, and at least one error/edge path handled. Name your pattern (planner/executor, ReAct, etc.) and make the control flow legible.

**A3. Grounding, trust & correctness.** Every material claim (the scenario's key quantities, dates, and options) is traceable to a specific record or event, and the design actively guards against hallucination. In particular, the critical numbers come from at least one **deterministic decision function the agent calls as a tool** rather than being generated by the model, and evidence/citations are surfaced to the user.

**A4. Engineering quality & runnability.** Reproducible end-to-end from your README via one documented path. Thoughtful structure, meaningful commit history, pinned dependencies, no committed secrets, and a **committed demo transcript/sample output** so reviewers can see it run without your API key. A lightweight test is a plus.

---

## Dimension B — Domain & Data Logic *(30%)*

**B1. Relational model fidelity.** All objects with correct primary keys and every foreign-key relationship captured, expressed as DDL/ERD, with correct cardinality — and clearly serving as the substrate your semantic layer is built on.

**B2. Context / semantic model quality.** A genuinely LLM-navigable knowledge layer, cleanly separated from the raw data, encoding the business semantics that define risk in this scenario (the meaning of the domain's key terms, how the relevant quantities aggregate) **and the business rules and preferences from the discovery interview** — richly enough that an agent can answer the scenario's core *"is X at risk, and why?"* question, and act on it the way the customer would want, without hard-coded joins.

**B3. Correct bottleneck detection.** A precise, well-explained detection that connects the relevant state, demand/obligations, and timing, and surfaces the causal event described in the scenario from the event log.

**B4. Resolution reasoning & trade-offs.** Multiple valid options weighed on relevant axes (e.g., cost, time, reliability, priority) — ideally via an explicit scoring/optimization function — using the levers the data actually provides, and **consistent with the customer's stated preferences, priorities, and constraints from the discovery interview**, leading to a defensible recommendation. There is no single correct answer — we assess the quality of your reasoning.

---

## Dimension C — Product Thinking *(30%)*

Assessed primarily from `blueprint.md`.

**C1. Architectural narrative & rationale.** A sharp, honest architecture story that explains the *why* behind your choices — including trade-offs you considered and rejected, and how you engineered trust and grounding.

**C2. Scalability reasoning.** A systems-level view of scaling from this toy dataset to millions of objects and many entities: cost, latency, and quality trade-offs, generalization to other process domains, and what you'd re-architect vs. reuse.

**C3. Strategic & executive value.** An executive-ready case framed for a CFO/CPO, tied to measurable KPIs with a rollout view — and a clear articulation of why this is categorically more than a better dashboard.

**C4. Communication & coherence.** Polished, precise, dual-audience writing (credible to both engineers and executives) that stays consistent with what you actually built.

---

*We read submissions with an AI-assisted first pass followed by human review. Structuring your repo so it's easy for both a human and an agent to navigate is a small part of the signal.*
